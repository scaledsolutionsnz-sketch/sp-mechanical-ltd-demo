About Shane

Reliable Mechanical
Work You Can Trust

SP Mechanical has been serving the Takaka and Golden Bay community with reliable, honest mechanical work for years. Shane's expertise covers everything from WOFs to full engine rebuilds, and in a remote area like Golden Bay, that kind of trusted, capable mechanic is worth its weight in gold.

When you bring your vehicle to SP Mechanical, you're dealing directly with Shane. No middlemen, no inflated charges, no surprises. Just straight talk and quality work done right the first time.

What We Do

Services We Offer

From a quick WOF to a full engine rebuild, if it's got an engine, Shane can sort it out.

WOF & Certification

Warrant of Fitness inspections carried out on-site. We'll let you know exactly what's needed and get you back on the road safely, with no unnecessary extras.

General Servicing

Regular servicing keeps your vehicle running reliably. Oil changes, filters, brakes, tyres, and full mechanical checks, all done properly, every time.

Engine Repairs

From head gaskets to full rebuilds, Shane handles complex engine work with the kind of care and precision that only comes from years of hands-on experience.

Electrical Diagnostics

Modern vehicles need modern diagnostic tools. We have the equipment and the know-how to track down electrical faults quickly and get things sorted.

4WD & Off-Road

Golden Bay's roads and landscapes demand capable 4WDs. Whether it's a lift kit, diff service, or general 4x4 maintenance, Shane knows these vehicles inside out.

Farm Equipment

Rural machinery keeps Golden Bay moving. SP Mechanical services tractors and farm equipment, helping local farmers stay productive through every season.
